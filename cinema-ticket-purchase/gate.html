    How to use this page offline
    1. Before the event (with internet): open this page, sign in, then press Update list. The counter "Tickets" shows how many tickets were downloaded. Press it again just before doors open so late approvals are included.
    2. At the gate (no internet needed): scan the QR code or search the name or ticket number. Each check-in is saved on this phone, and the "To sync" number goes up.
    3. Keep the page open or bookmarked. Open it once while online first, so the phone saves it. Use a normal browser window, not private mode.
    4. When the signal returns: the status at the top turns to Online and check-ins upload by themselves. You can also press Sync now. Wait until "To sync" shows 0.
    5. Several phones: each phone only knows its own check-ins until it syncs. After syncing, press Update list so it also sees the others. Until then a ticket already used on another phone can still scan as valid.
    6. After the event: sync first, then press Clear data to remove the list from the phone. Do not clear while "To sync" is above 0, or those check-ins are lost.
