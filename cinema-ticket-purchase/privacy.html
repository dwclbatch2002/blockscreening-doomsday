Privacy Notice

Avengers: Doomsday (2026) · Special Block Screening

Last updated: October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to sell you tickets and reserve your seats. We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We delete it after the event.

Who we are

This website is run by DWCL Batch 2002 for the Avengers: Doomsday block screening on December 19, 2026, at A. Bichara Silverscreens, Cinema 4. DWCL Batch 2002 is the party that decides how your information is used (the “personal information controller”).

Questions or requests: dwclbatch2002@gmail.com

What we collect, and why

  • Your name, email address and mobile numberTo record your order, contact you about it, and send your tickets.
  • Names of ticket holders (entered by the buyer, and may include children)To print each name on its ticket and reserve the seats.
  • Your payment proof (a screenshot or photo you upload)To confirm your payment and keep a record of what was received for the fundraiser.
  • Your seat choices and order referenceTo keep seats from being double-booked and let you reopen your order.

We do not ask for card numbers, bank passwords or government IDs. Payments are made outside this website, and you only upload the proof.

Who can see it

  • The DWCL Batch 2002 organizers who manage orders and payments.
  • Our technical service providers, who store and deliver the website: Supabase (database and file storage) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They process data for us and may keep ordinary server logs, such as your IP address.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with the cinema or anyone else for their own use.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies of its own. It does keep a few small items in your own browser so it can work:

  • Your order reference, so you can come back to your order page.
  • Your form draft, so details are not lost if you leave the page by mistake.
  • A note that you have seen the trailer, so it does not play again during the same visit.

These stay on your device and are needed for the site to work, so there is no cookie banner. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

The trailer is a YouTube video. When it loads, YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies and receive your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Orders that are not paid are removed automatically when the hold time runs out.
  • Paid orders, ticket holder names and payment proofs are kept for up to 7 days after the screening (until December 26, 2026) to handle questions, refunds and any disputes. After that they are deleted.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may object to how it is used. To do so, email dwclbatch2002@gmail.com with your order reference. We will reply as soon as we can. Ticket names can also be corrected once yourself, after approval, on your order page.

If you believe your information was mishandled, you may also contact the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
